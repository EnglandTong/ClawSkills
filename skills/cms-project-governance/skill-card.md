## Description: <br>
Turn vague or changing goals and legacy CMS project records into one compact, conflict-checked delivery state with clear outcomes, right-sized scope, bounded autonomy, alignment checks, delivery-class-aware evidence, and independent QA control. Includes anti-involution controls so governance removes uncertainty instead of producing documents. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Non-technical owners, product managers, and solo developers use this skill to decide whether to continue, cut, or archive a half-built project, size work before coding, and hold final acceptance authority with evidence that matches the delivery class. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Governance could grow into paperwork that outweighs working code. <br>
Mitigation: Anti-involution controls apply a document budget, price governance by uncertainty removed, and require subtraction before addition. <br>
Risk: Work could be accepted without the evidence its delivery class requires. <br>
Mitigation: Acceptance requires class-matched evidence; Accepted With Risk is only valid with an unlock condition, an owner, and a date. <br>
Risk: Legacy project state could be destroyed during bootstrap or rebaseline. <br>
Mitigation: History is archived by moving it, never by annotating it void in place; deletion, archive, reset, and rebaseline require an itemized dry run and explicit Owner approval. <br>
Risk: Sensitive operational data could reach project records or cloud services. <br>
Mitigation: Personal contact data, counterparty names, and contract or inspection details are never written into Active Packet, work orders, or QA decisions. <br>
